# 3. Use Case Scenarios — LegalEase

This document outlines the primary real-world personas, workflows, and operational scenarios supported by the LegalEase platform, directly aligning with the primary project specification (`LegalEase.pdf`).

---

## Scenario 1: Startup Founder Hiring a Full-Time Employee

- **Persona**: Alex Turner, Founder & CEO at Cyberdyne Systems Corp.
- **Goal**: Draft a comprehensive, professional Employment Contract for a Senior Systems Architect hire.
- **Workflow**:
  1. Alex opens the LegalEase web application at `http://localhost:8501`.
  2. In **Step 1**, selects **"Employment Contract"**.
  3. In **Step 2**, specifies parties:
     `John Smith (Employee), Cyberdyne Systems Corp. (Employer)`.
  4. In **Step 3**, enters tailored business terms separated by semicolons:
     `Position of Senior Systems Architect; Base annual salary of $130,000 paid bi-weekly; Standard comprehensive health insurance and 20 days paid annual leave; 30 days mutual written notice required for termination without cause; Strict non-solicitation of clients for 12 months post-employment`.
  5. In **Step 4**, selects the start date: `April 15, 2025`.
  6. In **Step 5**, uploads the corporate logo (`cyberdyne_logo.png`) and enters organization name `"Cyberdyne Systems Corp."`.
  7. Clicks **"Generate Document"**.
  8. Reviews the drafted contract in the dark-themed preview card, verifies compensation and non-solicitation clauses, clicks **"Download as .DOCX"**, and sends the Word file to HR for final sign-off.

## Scenario 2: Freelance Software Engineer Executing an NDA

- **Persona**: Jane Doe, Independent AI Engineer & Consultant.
- **Goal**: Execute a bilateral Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) prior to disclosing proprietary algorithm architectures to a prospective client.
- **Workflow**:
  1. Jane selects **"Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)"** from the document type dropdown.
  2. Inputs parties:
     `Apex Innovations LLC (Disclosing Party), Nexus Software Solutions (Receiving Party)`.
  3. Outlines confidential subject matter:
     `Confidential information includes proprietary algorithms, customer databases, and product roadmaps; Information shall not be disclosed to third parties without prior written consent; Confidentiality obligations shall endure for 3 years from effective date; Standard exclusions apply for public knowledge or court subpoenas`.
  4. Specifies effective date.
  5. Clicks **"Generate Document"**.
  6. Uses **"Click to Edit Document"** to refine a single clause, saves changes, and clicks **"Download as .PDF"** to obtain a signed-ready PDF complete with running header and signature lines.

## Scenario 3: Real Estate Landlord Drafting a Residential Lease

- **Persona**: Robert Miller, Residential Property Owner.
- **Goal**: Create a standard Residential Lease Agreement for a new tenant.
- **Workflow**:
  1. Robert chooses **"Residential / Commercial Lease Agreement"**.
  2. Enters:
     `Robert Miller (Landlord), Sarah Connor (Tenant)`.
  3. Inputs specific lease conditions:
     `Premises located at 742 Evergreen Terrace; Monthly rent of $1,800 due on the 1st of each month; Security deposit of $1,800 refundable upon move-out; Pets permitted subject to $250 non-refundable fee; Tenant responsible for utility charges`.
  4. Sets the commencement date.
  5. Generates the agreement, clicks **"Download as .TXT"** for quick archival in his tenant management records, and **"Download as .PDF"** for tenant execution.

## Scenario 4: Digital Agency Executing a Master Service Agreement

- **Persona**: Marcus Vance, Operations Director at TechNova Media.
- **Goal**: Formalize deliverables, milestone-based compensation, and IP transfer terms with a corporate client.
- **Workflow**:
  1. Marcus chooses **"Service Agreement"** (or **"Custom Document"**).
  2. Inputs roles:
     `TechNova Media LLC (Service Provider), Global Enterprise Inc. (Client)`.
  3. Defines milestones and IP transfer:
     `Phase 1 delivery within 30 days; Net 15 payment terms upon milestone approval; IP rights assigned only upon full receipt of final payment; Mutual 15 days written notice for termination`.
  4. Uploads company agency branding logo.
  5. LegalEase produces Word (.docx) and PDF versions containing an automatically generated terms table, running headers, and legal footers.
